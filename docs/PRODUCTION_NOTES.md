# Production Notes — The Last Cup

## Logline
Far from home, a young Ethiopian adult is drawn back to memories of a grandmother and the coffee ceremony that brought family together. By preparing coffee in the present, the protagonist carries that tradition forward.

## Creative intent
The coffee ceremony is the emotional anchor: roasting, brewing, pouring, waiting, and sharing become expressions of patience, care, and belonging. Warm earth tones and gentle camera movement support a reflective tone.

## Story structure
- Opening (shots 1–2): distance and memory; coffee's aroma triggers recollection.
- Connection (shots 3–6): grandmother, ritual, family gathering, and a remembered lesson.
- Return (shots 7–9): protagonist recreates the ritual and shares a cup.
- Closing (shot 10): title and disclosure.

## Runtime
Ten shots total 75 seconds. The exact duration is validated from the storyboard before rendering.

## Current status
The offline draft uses placeholder visuals, music, and silent narration so the pipeline can be tested without paid services or API credentials. Final-quality generative assets and a real Amharic voice performance are not included.
