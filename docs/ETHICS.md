# Ethical and Cultural Considerations

## Cultural respect
The Ethiopian coffee ceremony is a living cultural practice, not merely a visual motif. Treat people, objects, gestures, and settings with care. Where possible, consult people familiar with the traditions represented and invite feedback before publication.

## Consent and privacy
- Use fictional characters or obtain informed consent for identifiable people.
- Do not upload private family photographs, names, addresses, or personal stories to third-party services without permission.
- Avoid prompts implying that a real person said or did something they did not.

## Transparency
Clearly distinguish generated, edited, stock, recorded, and placeholder assets. This prototype's default frames are graphic cards, its score is a synthesized tone bed, and its narration track is silent. Do not describe these as AI-generated imagery, music, or speech.

## Accuracy and publishing
Review clothing, coffee equipment, language, gestures, and household details for cultural accuracy. Check licenses and terms for every model, font, music asset, and service. Include AI-use disclosure where required.
