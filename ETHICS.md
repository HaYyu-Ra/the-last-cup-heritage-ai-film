# Ethics, consent, licences and disclosure

## Consent gate
The starter storyboard describes fictional, AI-imagined characters. No real family member's face, likeness, or voice is intentionally reproduced, and no voice cloning is used. If the final film uses an identifiable real person, obtain and document their informed consent for generation and publication before creating or sharing that material. If a real person's voice is used or cloned, obtain explicit recorded consent for that use and confirm the spoken words are approved.

**Consent status:** No real-person likeness or voice assets are included in the current offline placeholder render. Reassess this status before publishing any replacement assets.

## Privacy
Do not place private names, exact addresses, family secrets, or sensitive personal information in prompts sent to third-party services. Use anonymized descriptions where possible.

## AI disclosure
The film must contain a visible end card: **“Created with AI tools”**. Enable the relevant AI-generated/altered-content disclosure when posting to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or another platform. Preserve provenance metadata and watermarks where supplied; do not remove them to misrepresent origin.

## Cultural respect
The coffee ceremony imagery and Amharic narration should be reviewed by someone familiar with the tradition and language. Generated images can misrepresent clothing, utensils, gestures, or context; correct or remove inaccuracies before public release.
