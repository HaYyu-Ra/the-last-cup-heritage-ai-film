# Design record — The Last Cup

## Audience and intent
**Audience:** Ethiopian families, young Ethiopian viewers, and diaspora audiences who connect with family rituals and memories.

**Viewing context:** Primarily phone viewing, with a horizontal version for desktop and family screens.

**Intent:** Leave viewers with warmth, nostalgia, and the feeling that heritage travels with them.

**Story question:** How might a familiar Ethiopian coffee ceremony help someone living far from home feel connected to family and cultural memory?

## Story choice
The coffee ceremony is the film's recurring motif. The 75-second arc moves from distance, through sensory memory and the grandmother's ritual, to the protagonist carrying the tradition into the present.

## Shot rationale
Every shot has a `reason` field in `storyboard.json`. The ten shots establish distance, trigger memory, introduce the ritual, show its gestures and community, recall a saying, return to the present, and resolve with continuity and belonging.
